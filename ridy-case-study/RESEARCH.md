# Ridy 2.0 · College Student app: desk research

Each finding below comes from a cited source. Anything not sourced in the case study gets a "Concept" label.

## Commuting
- More than 85% of US college students are commuters, meaning they don't live in campus housing. (Inside Higher Ed, 2020, citing NASPA/ACPA commuter research)
- 15.6% of undergraduates live on campus, 27.5% live with parents and 56.9% live off campus away from parents. (NCES NPSAS 2016, via ERIC ED606186, 2019)
- The average one-way student commute is about 46 minutes, from 18,500+ respondents. (StudentMoveTO, via The Varsity, 2020; Toronto data, not US)

## Lateness, stress and missed class
- 12% of students missed class or work because of a transportation problem. Only 8% of them used public transit assistance. (Hope Center, 2023–24 Student Basic Needs Survey, 91 colleges, published Feb 2025)
- 15% of students say they at least sometimes miss class because they lack reliable transportation. (Trellis, Student Financial Wellness Survey brief, 2023)
- 3 in 4 respondents had a car, yet nearly 1 in 5 car owners called it only "somewhat" or "not at all" reliable. (Trellis, 2023)

## Cost
- Owning and running a new car costs $11,577 a year, about $965 a month. (AAA Your Driving Costs, 2025). The 2026 edition says $12,863, but AAA changed its method, so the two numbers can't be compared directly.
- A student parking permit costs from $171 to $1,229 a year at Ohio State (WOSU, 2024) and up to $1,304 at UT Arlington (UTA PATS, 2023–24).
- The average US rideshare fare was $23.66 at the end of 2025, up about 10% in a year. Evening surge pricing adds 30–50%. (Gridwise via Yahoo Finance / Entrepreneur, 2026)

## Safety and parents
- 28% of students feel "not too safe" or "not at all safe" walking around their campus at night. (Inside Higher Ed Student Voice × College Pulse, 2021)
- 22% of women feel very safe in the area around campus, compared with 33% of men. (Student Voice, 2022, 2,000+ students at 100+ institutions)
- 67% of women named walking alone at night as their top safety concern, and 38% said safety worries limit their daily activities. (University Business, women's safety survey, July 2025)
- 32% of parents list student safety as a top concern. (EAB 2024 Parent Survey, via Inside Higher Ed, Aug 2024)
- 97% of adults aged 18–29 own a smartphone. (Pew Research Center, 2024)

## Shuttle ridership
- Rutgers Campus Buses: about 25.5M rides a year (Wikipedia, Rutgers Campus Buses)
- Texas A&M Transit: more than 7M passenger trips a year (TAMU Transportation, Transit Facts)
- Ohio State CABS: about 5M rides a year (Wikipedia, Campus Area Bus Service)

## Competitors
- TransLoc: 2.7★ iOS and 2.9★ Android. Several universities dropped it after price increases. (The Harvard Crimson, 2025)
- Passio GO: 1.3★ iOS and 2.1★ Android. Students complain about wrong arrival times and outdated routes. Harvard switched to Citymapper in 2026. (The Harvard Crimson, 2025 and 2026; Campus Times, 2024)
- DoubleMap: reviewers call the UI poor and say ETAs don't update when a bus pauses. (JustUseApp reviews)
- Google Maps transit and Uber/Lyft don't sync to class schedules, don't verify students, don't connect SOS to campus police, and have no shuttle boarding pass. (Feature comparison, Concept)
